JCM900 4100 100-Watt Guitar Head

SKU: #1742834618 | Model: #4100 | ★★★★★

Product Information

The Marshall JCM900 4100 is a legendary 100-watt all-tube guitar amplifier head that delivers the unmistakable Marshall tone with modern versatility. Originally launched in 1990 and now part of the Vintage Reissue series, this amp features two footswitchable channels—low-gain Channel A and high-gain Channel B—each with independent volume, gain, and reverb controls, plus a shared 4-band EQ for precise tonal shaping. Powered by three ECC83 preamp tubes and four 5881 power tubes, it offers rich, dynamic sound from sparkling cleans to saturated distortion. A built-in series effects loop with true bypass ensures seamless integration of pedals without compromising tone. The JCM900 also includes a power reduction switch, allowing players to toggle between 100W and 50W output for stage or studio use. Crafted in the UK, this amp is a durable, road-ready workhorse ideal for rock, metal, and blues players seeking iconic sound and performance.
