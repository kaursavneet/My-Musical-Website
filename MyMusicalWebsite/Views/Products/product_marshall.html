MG Gold Series 50W Combo w/ Digital FX

SKU: #4371923128 | Model: #MG50GFX | ★★★★☆

Product Information

The Marshall MG Gold Series MG50GFX is a powerful and versatile 50-watt combo amplifier designed for players who crave classic Marshall tone with modern flexibility. Featuring four programmable channels—Clean, Crunch, OD1, and OD2—this amp delivers everything from sparkling cleans to aggressive distortion. Its custom 12" speaker ensures bold, gig-worthy sound, while the built-in digital effects—including reverb, delay, chorus, flanger, phaser, and octave—expand your sonic palette. The MG50GFX also includes a dedicated delay control with four ambient modes and a built-in tuner for convenience. With an emulated headphone output, MP3/line input, and included two-button footswitch, it’s perfect for practice, studio, or stage. Wrapped in a sleek black and gold finish, this amp combines iconic style with cutting-edge performance.
