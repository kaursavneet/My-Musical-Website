RG450DXB Electric Guitar - White

Product Description

The Ibanez RG450DXB Electric Guitar in White is built for speed, precision, and bold tone, making it a top choice for modern rock and metal players. Featuring a sleek Meranti body with a striking white gloss finish and matching pickguard, this guitar delivers both visual impact and sonic power. Its ultra-fast Wizard III maple neck and bound Jatoba fingerboard with sharktooth inlays offer smooth playability across 24 jumbo frets. Equipped with Quantum pickups in an H-S-H configuration, the RG450DXB provides a wide tonal palette—from tight, aggressive lows to crisp highs. The Edge-Zero II tremolo bridge ensures expressive pitch control and stable tuning, while the mono-unit output jack enhances durability. Whether you're shredding solos or laying down heavy riffs, this guitar is engineered to perform.
