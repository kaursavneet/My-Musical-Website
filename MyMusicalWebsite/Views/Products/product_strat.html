The Fender American Vintage II 1957 Stratocaster in 2-Colour Sunburst is a masterfully crafted tribute to one of Fender’s most iconic designs. Built with a period-accurate alder body and finished in a premium gloss nitrocellulose lacquer, this Stratocaster captures the essence of mid-century tone and style. It features a comfortable 1957 “V”-shaped maple neck and a 7.25" radius maple fingerboard for authentic vintage feel and playability. A trio of Pure Vintage '57 single-coil pickups delivers sparkling highs, punchy mids, and warm lows, faithfully reproducing the legendary Strat sound. With vintage-style hardware, synchronized tremolo, and aged white appointments, this guitar is a true celebration of Fender’s golden era.
